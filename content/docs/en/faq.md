# Frequently Asked Questions

> This page has been generated by AI and may contain severe factual inaccuracies or mistakes.
> Use at your own risk.

Find answers to common questions about using Horenso SPACE.

## For Club Members

### How do I check my permissions?

Go to your club page and look at your role. You can see which permissions your role has by asking an admin or checking the role details in the club settings.

### Can I be in multiple clubs?

Yes! You can join as many clubs as you want. Each club will have its own separate settings, roles, and permissions.

### What happens if I miss submitting an absence report?

This depends on your club's policies. Contact your club leader or admin if you forgot to submit a report before an event.

### How do I know which events I need to attend?

Check the club calendar regularly. Events marked on the calendar are typically required unless stated otherwise. If you're unsure, ask your club leader.

### Can I edit an absence report after submitting it?

Currently, you cannot edit reports after submission. If you need to make changes, contact a club admin or leader who has the `canWriteReportReview` permission.

## For Club Admins

### Can I change someone's role after they join?

Yes! Go to **Config** → **Members**, find the member, and select a different role from the dropdown.

### What happens if an invitation expires?

Expired invitations can no longer be used. You'll need to create a new invitation if you want to invite more members.

### Can I invite multiple people with one link?

Yes! The same invitation link can be used by multiple people until it expires or is deleted. This makes it easy to share in group chats.

### How many admins should I have?

We recommend having 2-3 admins to ensure someone always has access to manage the club, but not so many that permissions and responsibilities become unclear.

### Can I delete a role that members are currently using?

You should first reassign all members from that role to a different role before deleting it. The system will prevent you from deleting a role that's still in use.

### How do I customize the absence report form?

Go to **Config** → **Absence Forms** to create custom form templates. You can add different field types (text, date, time, etc.) and assign forms to specific roles.

### What's the difference between canReadAllReports and canWriteReportReview?

- **canReadAllReports** - Allows viewing all absence reports but not adding reviews
- **canWriteReportReview** - Allows adding review comments and approval notes to reports (also grants read access)

Typically, leaders have both permissions, while admins may have only read access for oversight.

### Can members see who has what permissions?

Members can see role names but not the specific permissions assigned to each role, unless they have `canManageClub` access.

### How do I remove someone from my club?

Go to **Config** → **Members**, find the member, and click the remove or delete option. This will immediately revoke their access to the club.

### Can I have roles with the same permissions?

Yes, you can create multiple roles with identical permissions. This can be useful for organizational purposes (e.g., "Junior Leader" and "Senior Leader" both with leader permissions).

### What happens to absence reports if I delete a calendar event?

The absence reports remain in the system but will no longer be linked to a specific calendar event. It's best to keep events unless absolutely necessary to delete them.

## Technical Questions

### Is my data secure?

Yes. Horenso SPACE uses industry-standard security practices:

- Supabase PostgreSQL database with Row Level Security (RLS)
- Authentication through Supabase Auth
- HTTPS encryption for all data in transit

### Can I export my club data?

Data export features are planned for a future release. Currently, admins can view and manually copy data from the interface.

### Is there a mobile app?

Not yet, but the website is mobile-responsive and works well on phones and tablets. A dedicated mobile app may be developed in the future.

### Can I self-host Horenso SPACE?

Yes! Horenso SPACE is open source. See the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/zabackary/horenso-space) for self-hosting instructions.

### What browsers are supported?

Horenso SPACE works best on modern browsers:

- Chrome/Edge 90+
- Firefox 88+
- Safari 14+

## Billing and Pricing

### How much does Horenso SPACE cost?

Horenso SPACE is offered on a donation-based model. The hosted service is free to use, but we appreciate donations to help cover server costs and development.

### What if I can't afford to donate?

That's completely fine! Horenso SPACE is free for everyone. Donations are optional and help us maintain and improve the service.

### Can I get a receipt for my donation?

Contact us through the feedback form if you need a donation receipt.
