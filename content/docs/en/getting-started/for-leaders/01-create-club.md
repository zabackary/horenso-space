# Step 1: Creating Your Club

> This page has been generated by AI and may contain severe factual inaccuracies or mistakes.
> Use at your own risk.

Creating a club on Horenso SPACE is the first step toward better communication and organization for your team. This guide will walk you through the entire process, from initial setup to understanding your new club's structure.

## Before You Begin

Before creating your club, make sure you have:

- **A Horenso SPACE account** - If you don't have one yet, sign up at the homepage
- **Basic club information** - Your club's name, school or organization, and a brief description
- **Leadership authority** - You should be authorized to create and manage the club (president, captain, advisor, etc.)

Creating a club automatically grants you full administrative permissions, so ensure you're the right person for this role or plan to transfer ownership later.

## Creating Your Club

Once you're logged into Horenso SPACE, follow these steps to create your club:

### 1. Navigate to Club Creation

From the main application page, look for the **Create a Club** button. This is typically prominently displayed on your dashboard if you haven't joined any clubs yet. If you're already a member of other clubs, you'll find this option in the club selector dropdown menu in the top navigation bar.

### 2. Fill in Club Details

You'll be presented with a club creation form. Take your time to fill this out thoughtfully, as this information will be visible to all your members:

**Club Name**  
This is your club's official name. Choose something that clearly identifies your organization. Examples:

- "Roosevelt High School Marching Band"
- "University Robotics Team"
- "Community Theater Group"

Avoid abbreviations that outsiders might not understand. Remember, this name will appear in notifications, invitations, and member profiles.

**School or Organization**  
Enter the institution your club is associated with. This helps identify your club if multiple organizations use Horenso SPACE. Be specific:

- Instead of "High School", use "Lincoln High School"
- Instead of "University", use "State University"
- For community groups, use your city or region name

**Description** (Optional)  
While optional, a description helps new members understand your club's purpose. A good description includes:

- The club's mission or purpose
- Types of activities you do
- Meeting frequency (if regular)
- Any special requirements or expectations

For example: "We're a competitive robotics team that builds robots for VEX competitions. We meet three times a week after school and compete regionally throughout the year. Members learn engineering, programming, and teamwork skills."

### 3. Submit and Confirm

After reviewing your information, click **Create Club**. The system will:

1. Create your club's dedicated workspace
2. Assign you as the first administrator
3. Generate a default "Admin" role with full permissions
4. Set up your club's calendar
5. Initialize absence reporting system
6. Create file storage for your club

You'll be immediately redirected to your new club's homepage.

## Understanding Your New Club

Your newly created club comes with several pre-configured features:

### Default Admin Role

You automatically receive the "Admin" role, which includes all possible permissions:

- **Manage club** - Edit club settings, roles, and members
- **View all absence reports** - See every member's submitted reports
- **Review and respond to absence reports** - Approve or comment on reports
- **Submit absence reports** - Report your own absences
- **View club calendar events** - See the full event schedule
- **Create and edit calendar events** - Add practices, meetings, performances
- **Access club files** - View shared documents
- **Upload and manage club files** - Add, edit, and delete files

As the creator, you have complete control over every aspect of the club. This is the highest level of access, and you'll want to be thoughtful about who else you grant these permissions to.

### Your Club's Homepage

Your club homepage serves as the central hub for all activities. From here, members can:

- View upcoming calendar events
- Submit absence reports
- Access shared files (once you add them)
- See club announcements (future feature)

As an admin, you'll also see management options:

- **Config** - Access to roles, members, invitations, and forms
- **Calendar** - Full event creation and editing
- **Absences** - View and review all reports

### Initial Configuration

Your club is created with minimal settings, giving you flexibility to customize:

- **One role** - The default Admin role (yours)
- **Empty calendar** - Ready for you to add events
- **No members yet** - You're the only person in the club
- **Basic absence form** - A simple default form that you can customize

This clean slate approach lets you build exactly what your club needs without removing unnecessary defaults.

## What Happens After Creation?

After creating your club, you become responsible for:

1. **Setting up roles** - Define who can do what in your club
2. **Creating invitation links** - Generate ways for members to join
3. **Configuring forms** - Customize absence report requirements
4. **Adding calendar events** - Populate the schedule
5. **Inviting members** - Share links with your team

Don't worry about doing everything at once. You can configure these gradually as you get familiar with the system.

## Common Questions

**Can I change the club name later?**  
Yes, club admins can edit the club name, description, and organization at any time through the Config menu. However, try to choose a name you'll keep, as changing it might confuse members.

**Can I delete my club?**  
Club deletion is currently not available through the interface for safety reasons. Contact support if you need to permanently remove a club. You can, however, remove all members and stop using it.

**What if I made a mistake during creation?**  
All club information can be edited after creation except for the club's unique identifier (which members never see anyway). Go to Config → Club Settings to make changes.

**Can I have co-founders or multiple creators?**  
Only one person creates the club initially, but you can immediately create additional admins by setting up invitation links with the Admin role. Your co-leaders will have identical permissions.

**Is there a limit to how many clubs I can create?**  
There's no hard limit, but we recommend keeping it reasonable. Each club requires active management, so consider whether one club with sub-groups might work better than multiple separate clubs.
