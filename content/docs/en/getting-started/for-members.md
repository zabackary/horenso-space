# Getting Started for Members

> This page has been generated by AI and may contain severe factual inaccuracies or mistakes.
> Use at your own risk.

Welcome to your club on Horenso SPACE! This guide will help you get started as a club member.

## Joining Your Club

Your club leader will send you an invitation link. Here's how to join:

1. **Click the invitation link** from your leader
2. **Create an account** (or log in if you already have one)
   - Enter your email address
   - Set a password
   - Provide your full name
3. **Accept the invitation**
4. You're in! You'll see your club's home page

## Your Club Dashboard

After joining, you'll see:

- **Calendar** - Upcoming practices, performances, and events
- **Absences** - Where you submit and view your absence reports
- **Files** (if your role allows) - Shared club documents
- **Profile** - Your account settings

## Viewing the Calendar

The calendar shows all club events.

### How to Use It

1. Click **Calendar** in the sidebar
2. Browse by month, week, or day
3. Click on events to see details:
   - Event name and description
   - Date and time
   - Location
   - How many members will be absent

### Tips

- Check the calendar at least once a week
- Look ahead to plan your schedule
- Note any location changes
- Sync important events to your personal calendar

For more details, see [Using the Calendar](/docs/en/features/calendar).

## Submitting Absence Reports

If you can't attend an event, submit an absence report.

### How to Submit

1. Go to **Absences** → **Create Report**
2. Select the event you'll miss
3. Fill out the form:
   - Select the event date and time
   - Explain why you'll be absent
   - Answer any additional questions
4. Click **Submit**

### When to Submit

- **As early as possible** - Don't wait until the last minute
- **Before the event** - Submit reports in advance when possible
- **Emergency absences** - Submit as soon as you can, even if it's short notice

### What Happens After Submission

1. Your report is sent to club leaders
2. Leaders may add a review or approval
3. You'll see the status in **Absences** → **My Reports**
4. Leaders might contact you for more information

## Viewing Your Reports

See all your submitted absence reports:

1. Go to **Absences** → **My Reports**
2. Click on a report to view:
   - Event details
   - Your submission
   - Any reviews from leaders
   - Approval status

## Understanding Your Permissions

Your role determines what you can do. Common member permissions:

- ✅ **Submit absence reports** - Report when you can't attend events
- ✅ **View club calendar events** - See all scheduled activities
- ✅ **Access club files** - Download shared documents

To see your exact permissions, check your role in the club settings or ask a club admin.

For detailed permission explanations, see [Understanding Roles and Permissions](/docs/en/getting-started/roles).

## Accessing Club Files

If your role allows, you can access shared files:

1. Go to **Files** in the sidebar
2. Browse available documents
3. Download files you need
4. (Some roles can also upload files)

## Updating Your Profile

Keep your profile information current:

1. Click your profile picture in the top right
2. Select **Profile and settings**
3. Update:
   - Full name
   - Profile picture
   - Bio
   - School information
4. Save changes

## Best Practices

### Be Proactive

- Check the calendar regularly
- Submit absence reports early
- Read announcements from leaders
- Keep your profile information current

### Communication

- Respond promptly to questions from leaders
- Use the absence report system instead of informal messages
- Contact leaders if you have questions

### Responsibility

- Plan ahead when possible

- Follow club policies and guidelines

## Common Scenarios

### "I forgot to submit an absence report"

Submit it as soon as possible and explain the situation in the report. Contact your leader separately if it's urgent.

### "I can't find an event on the calendar"

Check that you're viewing the correct date range. If it's still missing, ask a club leader to verify the event exists.

### "I need to change a report I already submitted"

Currently, you cannot edit submitted reports. Contact a club leader to make changes or submit a new report with corrections.

### "I'm not sure if I need to report this absence"

When in doubt, submit a report. It's better to over-communicate than under-communicate. Check your club's specific policies.

## Troubleshooting

### Can't access the club

- Verify you used the correct invitation link
- Check that the invitation hasn't expired
- Contact your club leader for a new invitation

### Calendar not showing events

- Refresh the page
- Check you're viewing the correct month/week
- Verify you have calendar viewing permissions
- Ask a leader if events have been added yet

### Can't submit an absence report

- Check that you have the "Submit absence reports" permission
- Verify the form is loading correctly
- Try refreshing the page
- Contact an admin if the problem persists
