# Creating Roles and Inviting Members

> This page has been generated by AI and may contain severe factual inaccuracies or mistakes.
> Use at your own risk.

Once you've created your club, the next step is to set up roles and invite your members. This guide walks you through the entire process.

## Step 1: Create Your First Role

Roles define what members can do in your club. Start by creating roles that match your club's structure.

### Creating a Role

1. Go to your club page
2. Click **Config** in the sidebar
3. Select **Roles**
4. Click **Create New Role**
5. Fill in the role details:
   - **Name** - Choose a clear, descriptive name (e.g., "Practice Leader", "Member")
   - **Description** - Explain what this role is for
   - **Permissions** - Check the boxes for what this role can do

### Example Role Setup

Here's a typical setup for a school club:

#### Admin Role

- ✅ All permissions enabled
- Used for: Club president, faculty advisor

#### Leader Role

- ✅ canReadAllReports
- ✅ canWriteReportReview
- ✅ canReadCalendar
- ✅ canWriteCalendar
- Used for: Section leaders, team captains

#### Member Role

- ✅ canWriteOwnReport
- ✅ canReadCalendar
- ✅ canReadFiles
- Used for: Regular club members

## Step 2: Generate an Invitation Link

Once you have roles set up, you can invite members to join your club.

### Creating an Invitation

1. Go to **Config** → **Invites**
2. Click **Create New Invitation**
3. Choose the **role** you want to assign to invited members
4. Set an **expiration date** (recommended: 7-30 days)
5. Click **Create**

### Sharing the Invitation

1. Copy the invitation link
2. Share it with your members via:
   - Email
   - Chat group (LINE, Discord, Slack)
   - QR code (for in-person invites)

### Security Tips

- ⚠️ Set expiration dates on all invitations
- ⚠️ Delete invitations after your members have joined
- ⚠️ Don't share invitation links publicly
- ⚠️ Create separate invitations for different roles

## Step 3: Members Join

When a member clicks your invitation link:

1. They'll see the club name and role they'll be assigned
2. They create an account or log in
3. They're automatically added to your club with the specified role

## Step 4: Manage Your Members

After members join, you can manage them from **Config** → **Members**.

### What You Can Do

- **View all members** - See who's in your club and their roles
- **Change roles** - Promote members or adjust permissions
- **Remove members** - Remove inactive or departed members

### Editing a Member's Role

1. Go to **Config** → **Members**
2. Find the member you want to update
3. Click their name or the edit icon
4. Select a new role from the dropdown
5. Save changes
