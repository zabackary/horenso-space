# Understanding Roles and Permissions

> This page has been generated by AI and may contain severe factual inaccuracies or mistakes.
> Use at your own risk.

Roles are the foundation of how Horenso SPACE manages access control in your club. Each member is assigned a role that determines what they can and cannot do.

## What are Roles?

A **role** is a set of permissions that defines what actions a member can perform. Common examples include:

- **Admin** - Full control over club settings and members
- **Leader** - Can manage reports and review submissions
- **Member** - Basic access to submit reports and view calendars

## Permission Types

### Report Permissions

- **canWriteOwnReport** - Submit absence reports
- **canReadAllReports** - View all absence reports
- **canWriteReportReview** - Review and respond to absence reports

### Calendar Permissions

- **canReadCalendar** - View club calendar events
- **canWriteCalendar** - Create and edit calendar events

### File Permissions

- **canReadFiles** - Access club files
- **canWriteFiles** - Upload and manage club files

### Management Permissions

- **canManageClub** - Manage club (full administrative access to roles, members, and club settings)

## Understanding Key Concepts

### What are Reports?

**Reports** (or absence reports) are formal submissions that members create to notify the club about their absence from events or activities. Each report includes:

- Event details (date, time, activity)
- Reason for absence
- Custom form fields (configured by club admins)

### What are Reviews?

**Reviews** are feedback or approval notes that leaders add to absence reports. They help track:

- Whether the absence was approved
- Any follow-up actions needed
- Communication between members and leaders

### What are Roles?

A **role** is a set of permissions that defines what actions a member can perform in the club.

## Report Review Hierarchy

The report system follows a hierarchical structure:

1. **Member submits a report** - Any member with `canWriteOwnReport` can submit an absence report
2. **Leader reviews the report** - Members with `canWriteReportReview` can add reviews to reports
3. **Admin oversight** - Members with `canReadAllReports` can see all submissions

## For Club Members

As a regular club member, you'll typically have these permissions:

- `canWriteOwnReport` - Submit your own absence reports
- `canReadCalendar` - View upcoming events and activities
- `canReadFiles` - Access shared club files

You can check your permissions by viewing your role in the club settings.

## For Club Admins

### Setting Up Roles

When creating roles for your club:

- **Start simple** - Create basic roles first (Admin, Leader, Member)
- **Grant minimum permissions** - Only give permissions that are truly needed
- **Use descriptive names** - Make role names clear ("Practice Leader" vs "Role 1")
- **Test before rolling out** - Create a test role and verify permissions work as expected
