# Welcome to Horenso SPACE

Horenso SPACE helps clubs, teams, and organizations manage communication, track absences, and stay organized. Whether you're running a school club, sports team, or community group, this guide will help you get started.

## Getting Started

New to Horenso SPACE? Choose your guide:

- **[For Club Leaders](/docs/en/getting-started/for-leaders)** - Complete setup guide for admins creating and managing clubs
- **[For Club Members](/docs/en/getting-started/for-members)** - Quick start guide for members joining a club
- **[Understanding Roles and Permissions](/docs/en/getting-started/roles)** - Learn how roles control access
- **[Creating Roles and Inviting Members](/docs/en/getting-started/invites)** - Detailed guide for setting up your club structure

## Key Features

### Absence Reports

Submit and track member absences for events and activities.

- Members submit reports explaining why they'll be absent
- Leaders review and approve absence requests
- Everyone stays informed about attendance

### Calendar

Keep track of practices, performances, meetings, and events.

- **[Using the Calendar](/docs/en/features/calendar)** - View and manage club events

### Roles and Permissions

Control who can do what in your club with customizable roles.

## Common Workflows

### For Club Admins

1. Create your club
2. Set up roles (Admin, Leader, Member)
3. Create invitation links
4. Invite members to join
5. Configure absence report forms
6. Add events to the calendar

### For Members

1. Join via invitation link
2. View the calendar for upcoming events
3. Submit absence reports when needed
4. Check for updates from leaders

## What is "Horenso"?

"Horenso" (報連相) is a Japanese business term combining three concepts:

- **報告 (Houkoku)** - Reporting
- **連絡 (Renraku)** - Communicating
- **相談 (Soudan)** - Consulting

It represents the core of good team communication—exactly what this platform helps you achieve!
